Editorial web design mockup of a "Selected Projects" portfolio grid section for George Efesopoulos, a UX/UI product designer & developer. Goal: showcase 4-6 case study cards with visual hierarchy that signals craft and makes the work itself the centrepiece.

Brand: Background warm olive cream #E8E0CA. Card surface #DDD3B9. Text primary deep coffee brown #3A2D18. Muted text #6F6852. Accent espresso #2A1B08. Border hairlines #CDC4A8. Primary font Inter sans. Display/heading font Instrument Sans (variable width, slightly condensed). Style: editorial, considered, restrained whitespace. Reference: It's Nice That meets Monocle.

Layout: Full-width section, 120px vertical padding. Top-left: section label in small uppercase wide-tracked sans "SELECTED PROJECTS". Beneath it on the same row, a small pill-row of filter tabs (All, Design, WordPress, Next.js) rendered as simple underline-active tabs - no filled buttons, no rounded pills. Below the header row, a staggered editorial bento grid: top row has one large featured card spanning ~60% width (tall, portrait crop thumbnail with a big project title overlaid at the bottom in large bold Instrument Sans, on a dark scrim) beside a narrower right column containing two stacked smaller cards (landscape thumbnail + title + one-line category tag below). Second row: three equal-width cards with a horizontal thumbnail strip at top, then category tag in tiny uppercase, then project name, then one short descriptor line. Cards have square corners (0-2px radius), single hairline #CDC4A8 border, no drop shadows.

Mood: editorial portfolio, magazine-quality, mature and considered. The work thumbnails should feel like windows into real projects - prominent, not shrunk. NO rounded SaaS cards. NO coloured icon boxes. NO drop shadows. NO gradient overlays except on the large featured card scrim. NO em-dashes. Lots of negative space between cards. The overall feeling should be closer to a print portfolio than a SaaS product page.
